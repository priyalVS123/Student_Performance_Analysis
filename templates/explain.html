Explain Prediction

Understanding which factors influence student performance predictions

🔍 Explainable AI using SHAP

The StudentIQ model uses a Random Forest regression model to estimate a student's final grade. SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) helps explain which features contribute most strongly to the model's predictions.

Feature Importance

Features with larger SHAP values have a stronger influence on the model's predictions.

📚 Previous Failures

Previous academic failures are one of the strongest factors influencing the model's prediction of final performance.

📅 Absences

Absence levels have a strong influence on the model's predicted academic performance.

⏱ Study Time

Weekly study time contributes to the model's understanding of a student's academic performance.

❤️ Health

Reported health status is another factor considered by the predictive model.

👥 Social Activity

Variables such as going out and social activity can contribute to the model's prediction.

🎓 Education Factors

Parental education and educational support variables also contribute to the model's predictions.

Important: SHAP explains how features influence the machine-learning model's predictions. It does not prove that a feature directly causes a student's academic outcome.
